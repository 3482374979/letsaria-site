1. What we process, why, and for how long

We process only what we need for the purposes below and do not use it for other purposes without a further legal basis.

1.1 Processed to provide the Service (no separate consent)

We process the following to perform our contract with you (to provide the Service you asked for). Server access logs are processed for our legitimate interest in keeping the Service secure, and records that a law requires us to keep are processed to comply with that legal obligation. AI answer ratings are processed only when you tap them, and only the items below, for our legitimate interest in improving the quality of answers (for users in Korea, Article 15(1)(6) of the Personal Information Protection Act); if you turn off Settings → AI → “Answer Rating Buttons” (on by default), the buttons disappear and nothing more is sent. To have ratings you already sent deleted right away, contact us as described in Section 10.

1.2 Processed with your consent (AI features)

Aria is an assistant built on generative AI, so AI processing is an essential part of the Service. Before anything is sent to an AI provider, the app shows which AI provider receives which information and when (including the destination country, the retention period and what happens if you decline) and asks for your explicit consent. New users consent in the AI step of initial setup; existing users who have not yet consented (including those who had AI features off in an earlier version) see a consent screen the next time they open the app. Nothing is sent to an AI provider before you consent.

1.3 Processing our server does automatically

Our server does not send your content to external AI providers on its own (there are no automatic server summaries or tags). Content is sent to an AI provider only by the app, and only after you consent to AI processing (Section 1.2). Our server records your account’s most recent AI consent decision and when it was made (Section 1.1), and uses it only to confirm consent and to refuse AI requests from an account whose consent has been withdrawn.

1.4 Device features (permissions)

  • Location
    • Location permission: From app version 1.1, the app requests location permission only “While Using the App”, and location reminders work with that permission. App version 1.0 also asked for “Always” permission when you created a location reminder (Section 14).
    • Location reminders: When you add a location reminder to a task or routine, app version 1.1 or later stores and syncs only the place name with the reminder on our server (the name of a place you searched for and chose, an area name, or “Current location” / “Selected location”) (Section 1.1); the place’s coordinates are stored only on that device. On your other devices, choose the place again for the reminder to fire there. Version 1.0 stored and synced the place’s name together with its coordinates. Your device decides when a reminder fires; your movements and real-time location are not sent to our server.
    • Attaching your current location in a chat: If you choose “Current location” from the attachment menu in an AI chat, app version 1.1 or later includes only the area name of your device’s position (neighborhood and district) in that message, stores it with your chat history on our server, and sends it to Anthropic to generate the reply. The coordinates are stored only on that device and are used only for its map preview and location reminders; your other devices show the area name only. Version 1.0 sent the place name together with the coordinates. If you do not choose it, no location is included in the chat.
    • Coordinates sent by app version 1.0: Coordinates that version 1.0 sent with chat attachments and location reminders may remain on our server until you ask us to delete them (Section 10) or delete your account. Version 1.1 or later does not include coordinates from earlier chats in AI requests or search embeddings, and when that device syncs such a chat or reminder again, the server copy is overwritten without coordinates.
    • Weather: To show weather, your device sends your current location rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km) directly to a weather service: Apple’s weather service (WeatherKit) in app version 1.1 or later, and Open-Meteo in version 1.0. These coordinates are not sent to our server and we do not collect them. To show an area name (such as a city name) on the dashboard weather card, your device also sends the same rounded coordinates to Apple’s map service and receives the name of that area. It does this only when it does not have a name yet or when the rounded coordinates or the app language change; the name is stored only on your device and is not sent to our server or to AI providers. AI providers receive a weather summary sentence, not coordinates. A weather card shown in an AI chat (weather values, and a place name if the AI named one) is stored with that chat history.
  • Calendar: The app shows events from your device calendar and adds or changes events you ask for. Calendar events themselves are not synced to our server. However, the title, time and place of events you look up, add or change in an AI chat are stored with that chat history and sent to Anthropic.
  • Reminders (read-only · app version 1.1.1 or later): Only when you use “Import Apple Reminders”, the app reads the reminder lists you choose and turns them into tasks. It only reads your reminders and never changes or deletes them. To organize the items and show them in a preview, the app sends to Anthropic through our server the title, the beginning of the notes (up to 200 characters) and the list name of each incomplete reminder in the chosen lists that has not been imported yet, whether it already has a due date or repeat, and the names of your task categories. Locations, URLs and alert times are not sent to the AI. Only the items you select in the preview are saved and synced as tasks (Section 1.1 — a reminder’s URL goes into the task notes, and only the place name of its location is synced; coordinates stay on that device), and which reminders have already been imported is recorded only on that device to prevent duplicates. If AI is unavailable, reminders are imported as they are, without AI.
  • Health (read-only): Step count and sleep data are used only to show, in the Weekly Briefing, how they relate to the tasks you complete, and the calculation happens only on your device. They are never sent to our server or to AI providers (they are not included in the AI-written text of the Weekly Briefing either), never used for advertising, and nothing is written to the Health app.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: Used for voice memos, dictation and recording summaries. Voice memo recordings stay on your device; only the transcript is synced. Transcription runs on your device; if your device cannot transcribe on its own, audio may be sent to Apple’s speech recognition service, which is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.
    • Recording summaries (app version 1.1.2 or later): When chat dictation runs long, or when you tap “Start Recording” or “Record and Summarize”, the app keeps transcribing for up to 2 hours even while the screen is off or you use other apps (your device shows its microphone-in-use indicator). Recording summaries work only on devices that can transcribe on the device itself (nothing is sent to Apple’s servers), and audio is never saved. The transcribed text stays only on that device (excluded from sync and backup) until you finish the summary, move it to the text field, clear it or sign out. When you tap “Summarize”, the transcribed text, when the recording started, its length and the title of the event you linked are sent through our server to Anthropic to produce a summary, decisions and suggested to-dos. A one-line reply is saved and synced with your chat history; the summary card is stored only on that device and may be included in AI requests when you ask your next question in the same chat. If a calendar event is in progress when you start recording (and you have allowed calendar access), the app links it on the device, and the summary is added below that event’s notes only when you tap “Add to Notes”.
  • Camera and photos: Used to take or choose photos to attach. The app does not request access to your whole photo library; it processes only the photos you choose.
  • Share extension: When you pick Aria in another app’s share sheet, Aria receives the text or links you shared and, from app version 1.1.2, photos (up to 5 at a time) or a PDF (1). Photos and PDFs wait in a temporary folder on your device until you open Aria, then become attachments and the temporary copy is deleted; reading the text and finding dates happen on your device. The attachments are stored and synced like other attachments (Section 1.1), and their text may be sent to AI providers for automatic organizing and the search index (Section 1.2). When you tap “Turn what I shared into events and tasks”, the text your device read (not the image itself) is sent to Anthropic to suggest events and tasks.
  • Notifications: Used for reminders, briefings, check-ins and a notice before a free trial ends (app version 1.1.2 or later). Your remote push token is not sent to our server.
  • Alarms (iPhone and iPad app version 1.1.2 or later): Used to ring tasks you set to “Ring as Alarm” like an alarm, even in Silent mode or a Focus. Alarm settings are stored only on that device and are not sent to our server (when an alarm rings, the task title appears on the Lock Screen).

1.5 Information kept only on your device (we do not collect it)

Mood check-in scores and daily mood records, voice recordings, transcribed recording text before it is summarized, D-days, photos you add to a day on the calendar (app version 1.1.2 or later), health data, the name received when you sign in with Apple (used to address you in the app), nickname, interests and tone settings, and app settings are stored only on your device. However, the items listed in Section 1.2 (nickname, interests, tone; weekly average mood score; a mood you ask the AI to log in a chat; D-days; recording text you ask to summarize) are sent to Anthropic with your AI requests. Result cards for past chats, the list of web sources found (page title and address) and option cards, the overnight-recap step list and recording summary cards are also stored only on that device and are not synced (the reply text is saved and synced as part of the chat history).

1.6 When your device connects to other services directly

  • Link previews: To fetch the title and preview image of a link you save, your device connects directly to that website, which receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address.
  • Weather: Your device requests weather directly from a weather service (Apple WeatherKit in app version 1.1 or later; Open-Meteo in version 1.0) as described in Section 1.4 (see Section 5).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store payments and subscription management, the speech recognition fallback, Apple’s map service (showing maps and searching for places, finding the place name for a location reminder or a location you attach in a chat, and the area name on the weather card), and Apple’s weather service (WeatherKit — weather data in app version 1.1 or later) are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Meta (iPhone and iPad app version 1.1 or later): The ad measurement information in Section 1.7 is sent by your device directly to Meta’s servers. Nothing is sent if you turn off Settings → Privacy → “Ad Measurement”.

2. Deletion

  1. Principle: We delete personal information without delay when it is no longer needed, for example when the retention period ends or the purpose has been achieved.
  2. Deleting your account: In Settings → Account, tap “Delete account” (if signed in with Apple) or “Delete all my data” (guest). Your account and its data on our server (all synced data, attachments, subscription verification records, usage counts and answer ratings) are deleted immediately, your Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked, and the data on that device is erased. This cannot be undone.
  3. Backups: Deleted data may remain in disaster-recovery database backups for up to 8 days until those backups are automatically replaced. Backups are used only for disaster recovery.
  4. Items you delete individually: They are removed from your device immediately (or after the trash period, where the app has a trash). Our server keeps a deletion marker so your other devices stay in sync. Currently the content of those items also remains on the server until you delete your account; we do not use it for anything other than sync. To have it erased from the server now, delete your account or contact us (Section 10).
  5. Signing out: Signing out of your Apple account erases Aria data on that device. Your data stays in your account on the server and is restored when you sign in again. Mood records, D-days and photos added to a day on the calendar have no copy on our server, so instead of being erased they are kept on that device for each account and restored when you sign in to the same account again; they are deleted when you delete your account. Content already shown in a Home Screen widget may remain until the widget refreshes.
  6. Guest accounts: A guest account can be reached only from the device that created it. Before deleting the app, use Settings → Account → “Delete all my data” to erase your server data. If the app is deleted first, we hold nothing that links that guest account to you, so we may be unable to find and delete it even if you ask.
  7. Method: Electronic data is deleted from our database and storage so that it cannot be restored. We do not keep personal information on paper.
  8. Retention required by law: Subscription payments and receipts are handled and kept by Apple; we do not hold payment methods or payment amounts. Records of consumer complaints and disputes that we receive by email are kept for 3 years under Article 6(1)(4) of the Enforcement Decree of the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, separately from other information, even if you delete your account, and then deleted. If any other law requires us to keep information, we likewise keep it separately for the period required and then delete it.

7. Security

  • Organizational: access to personal information limited to the minimum number of people.
  • Technical: encryption of all data in transit (TLS), per-account access control (row-level security), restricted access to server administration credentials and keys, encryption of stored data, private storage for attachments, retention of server access logs (up to 7 days).
  • Physical: data is kept in cloud data centers operated by our provider (Supabase), whose security programs cover physical access control. We do not run our own server rooms.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the authorities of a breach as required by law.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

  • The app does not use cookies, does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • The iPhone and iPad app (version 1.1 or later) includes the Meta SDK to measure our ads (Section 1.7). The SDK creates a random ID for each app install, stores it on your device, and sends app opens and free-trial and subscription starts to Meta. You can turn it off in Settings → Privacy → “Ad Measurement”. We also learn which ads installs came from through Apple’s SKAdNetwork (Apple aggregates this so that individuals cannot be identified). The Mac app and app version 1.0 do not include this SDK, and we use no analytics or advertising SDK other than the Meta SDK.
  • To keep you signed in, the app stores an authentication token on your device. It is strictly necessary and is removed when you sign out or delete your account.
  • The website that hosts this policy does not use cookies or analytics. Its hosting provider (GitHub, Inc.) may keep ordinary access logs such as IP addresses for security and operations.
  • Behavioral information: We do not show ads in the app, and we do not use your content or your activity in the app (tasks, chats, searches, etc.) for advertising. However, the app opens and subscription starts that the Meta SDK sends may count as information about your app activity, and Meta may use it to improve ad delivery under its own terms and policies (what is collected, how, why, for how long and how to opt out: Sections 1.7 and 3).
