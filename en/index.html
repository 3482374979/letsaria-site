Tasks and events, sorted.

Aria is an AI assistant that turns one sentence into tasks and events, and shows your whole day on one screen when you ask.

Free to start on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

Mornings:
only what matters.

Your briefing covers what you finished yesterday and what to start first today, in two or three sentences. You can listen to it, too.

  • The Today dashboard lays out your day on a timeline and points to what fits the free time between events.
  • In the evening, review what is left and what slipped, then reschedule everything at once.

Tasks that fit
your free time.

Aria suggests what fits before your next event. Take a suggestion and it goes straight onto your calendar, with Undo right there.

  • Organize with repeating tasks, subtasks and categories, or sort them in an Eisenhower matrix of important and urgent.
  • Tasks that can’t be missed ring as alarms, even in Silent mode or a Focus. Set deadline reminders and location reminders, too.

Tasks and calendar,
together.

Events on top, the day’s tasks below, and free time marked in between. Drag a task onto the timetable to give it a time.

  • Switch between Day, 3-Day, Week and Month.
  • See your device calendar events alongside, and import Apple Reminders.
  • Write “tomorrow at 3 pm” and Aria understands.

Your notes,
connected.

Notes, links and files gather in Scrap, and Synapse maps the people, topics and ideas in them.

  • Search by meaning, even when the words differ, and jump to anything on the map.
  • What you tell Aria carries into later chats. View, edit or delete those memories in Settings.

Only after you agree,
only what is needed.

  • When you start, Aria first explains what goes where, and uses AI only after you agree.
  • Chat, briefings, automatic organizing and Synapse use Anthropic (Claude). Search by meaning uses OpenAI embeddings. You can withdraw consent anytime in Settings › Info.
  • Only what a request needs is sent, over an encrypted connection, and never your account identifier or email. Aria and its AI providers do not use your data to train AI models.
  • There are no ads in the app. You can start without signing in, and delete your account and data right in the app.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. You can download it free from the App Store, and the basics, like managing tasks and events, are free. AI questions are free up to 20 a day, refilled every day.

Do I have to sign in?

You can start right away without signing in. Sign in with Apple and your devices share the same record.

Is my data used to train AI?

No. Aria and its AI providers do not use your data to train AI models. What is sent and where is described in the AI Notice.

What about my existing calendar and reminders?

Aria shows your device calendar events alongside your own, and can import your Apple Reminders lists. Importing only reads your reminders and never deletes or changes them. Need help? Write to Support.
