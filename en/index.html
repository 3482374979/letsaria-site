Just say it

One sentence.
Tasks and events.

“Report by Friday, team meeting tomorrow at 3 pm.” Say it or type it, and Aria turns it into one task and one event.

  • Say a lot at once and Aria splits it into task suggestions. Tap Add all to keep them.
  • When you need fresh information, Aria searches the web and answers with sources.
  • Record a meeting or lecture to get a transcript, a summary and to-dos.

Morning briefing · Dashboard

Mornings:
only what matters.

Your briefing gives today’s schedule and what to do first in two or three sentences. You can listen to it, too.

  • Tap Start today to begin your first task with a timer. The time left shows on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • The Today dashboard lays out your day on a timeline. Prefer a graph? Choose it when you edit widgets.
  • In the evening, review what is left and what slipped, then reschedule everything at once. A weekly briefing looks back when the week ends.

Tasks · Calendar

Tasks and calendar,
together.

Move between Today, Upcoming, Someday and Inbox lists and Day, 3-Day, Week and Month calendars, and see tasks and events side by side.

  • Drag a task onto the timetable to give it a time, and drag events to move them.
  • Write “tomorrow at 3 pm” and Aria understands.
  • Sort tasks with an Eisenhower matrix: four boxes of important and urgent.
  • See your device calendar events alongside, and import Apple Reminders.

Subtasks · Reminders

Big tasks, small steps.
Reminders that stick.

Break a big task into subtasks. Set the due date and reminder right below.

  • Check off subtasks right from the list.
  • Tasks that can’t be missed ring as alarms, even in Silent mode or a Focus.
  • Set reminders for each stage of a deadline, and for when you arrive at or leave a place.
  • Slide a ruler to pick the alarm time and date in one go.

Routines

Routines,
step by step.

Turn what you do every day into a routine, and your streak builds.

  • A timer for each step leads you to the next one.
  • Check routines off right from a widget, and look back on the week in your weekly briefing.

Photo and screenshot to plan

From a photo
to a plan.

Share a flyer photo, a screenshot of a notice or a PDF schedule with Aria. It finds the dates and suggests events and reminders.

  • Share it to Aria and it sorts the content into events, tasks and scraps. You can also save it as is to your scraps.
  • Aria can look at photos you attach and turn them into tasks, even on the free plan.

Memory · Synapse

Your notes,
connected.

Keep notes, links and files in one place with Scrap. Synapse maps the people, topics and ideas in them.

  • Search by meaning even when the words differ, and jump straight to anything on the Synapse map.
  • People in your Synapse appear on the dashboard under People, with their related events and tasks.
  • Time Capsule brings back this day from a week to a year ago.
  • What you tell Aria carries into later chats, and you can view, edit and delete memories in Settings.

Widgets · Shortcuts

Before you open the app,
on your Home Screen.

Put task, routine, D-day and monthly calendar widgets on your Home Screen and Lock Screen, and finish tasks right from a widget.

  • Reach Aria from Control Center, the Action button, Siri and Shortcuts.
  • Press and hold the app icon for the Ask Aria and Add task quick actions.

Privacy · AI

Only after you agree,
only what is needed.

  • When you start, Aria first explains what goes where, and uses AI only after you agree.
  • Chat, briefings, automatic organizing and Synapse use Anthropic (Claude). Search by meaning uses OpenAI embeddings. You can withdraw consent anytime in Settings › Info.
  • Only what a request needs is sent, over an encrypted connection, and never your account identifier or email. Aria and its AI providers do not use your data to train AI models.
  • There are no ads in the app. You can start without signing in, and delete your account and data right in the app.

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. You can download it free from the App Store, and every non-AI feature is free. AI questions are free up to 20 a day, refilled every day.

Do I have to sign in?

You can start right away without signing in. Sign in with Apple and your devices share the same record.

Is my data used to train AI?

No. Aria and its AI providers do not use your data to train AI models. What is sent and where is described in the AI Notice.

What about my existing calendar and reminders?

Aria shows your device calendar events alongside your own, and can import your Apple Reminders lists. It only reads Reminders and never deletes or changes them. Need help? Write to Support.
