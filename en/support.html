Getting the most out of Aria

From app version 1.1.6, the tips that used to be in the app’s Settings live here.

  • Share sheet: Pick “Aria” in the Safari or Notes share sheet and it’s filed automatically.
  • Widgets: See today’s tasks and deadlines at a glance — Home Screen widgets on iPhone and iPad, desktop and Notification Center widgets on Mac.
  • Voice input: Speak into the mic in the chat field and Aria writes it down. On iPhone and iPad, add “Aria Recording” to Control Center to start right from the Lock Screen.
  • Use with Siri: Say “Ask Aria”, “What's on today in Aria” or “Read my Aria briefing” to Siri — no need to open the app. They’re in the Shortcuts app too.
  • Clipboard suggestions (iPhone and iPad): Copy a link or search text and Aria asks whether to put it in the chat.
  • Routines: Turn what you do every day into a routine and keep the streak.

Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

  • iPhone or iPad: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Aria → Cancel Subscription
  • Mac: App Store → your name → Account Settings → Subscriptions → Manage → Aria → Cancel Subscription
  • During a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged. App versions 1.1.2–1.1.4 send a reminder 2 days before the trial ends if you allow notifications (unless auto-renewal is turned off); from version 1.1.5 the app no longer sends this reminder. Even if you do not get the reminder, you will be charged unless you cancel during the trial.
  • Deleting your account in the app does not cancel your subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple processes App Store payments, so refunds are requested from Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, choose the purchase and submit a request. See Section 11 of our Terms of Service.

How do I use the same data and subscription on another device?

On each device, open Aria and go to Settings → Account → Sign in with Apple using the same Apple Account. If your subscription does not appear, tap “Restore purchases” on the subscription screen.

How do I turn calendar, reminders or notification access back on?

Once you choose “Don’t Allow”, your device won’t ask again for Aria. Turn access back on in these places — it takes effect as soon as you return to Aria.

  • iPhone or iPad, Calendars: Settings app → Apps → Aria → Calendars → “Full Access”
  • iPhone or iPad, Reminders: Settings app → Apps → Aria → Reminders → “Full Access”
  • iPhone or iPad, Notifications: Settings app → Apps → Aria → Notifications → turn on “Allow Notifications”
  • Mac, Calendars: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → turn on Aria
  • Mac, Reminders: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Reminders → turn on Aria
  • Mac, Notifications: System Settings → Notifications → Aria → turn on “Allow notifications”

From app version 1.1.5, the “Permissions” section in Aria’s settings (iPhone or iPad: Settings → Connections; Mac: Settings → Sync) shows all three and takes you straight to the right screen when you tap a row that’s off. If Calendars is set to “Add Events Only”, Aria can’t read your existing events, so choose “Full Access”.

Can I switch AI off?

There is no setting that stops AI features on their own while you keep using the rest of the app. Aria is an assistant built on generative AI, so AI processing is at the core of the Service. You can, however, withdraw your AI consent at any time.

  • First consent: before anything is sent to an AI provider, the app explains what is sent where and asks for your consent (new users during initial setup; existing users who have not yet consented, on a consent screen the next time they open the app). Nothing is sent before you consent, and without consent you cannot use Aria.
  • Withdrawing consent (app version 1.1 or later): go to Settings → Info → “Withdraw AI Consent” (version 1.1.5 or earlier: Settings → AI → “Withdraw AI Consent”) and confirm. Your records, such as tasks, notes and chats, are kept, and you’ll see the consent screen until you agree again.
  • Withdrawing consent (earlier versions): email our privacy officer (support@letsaria.com). You can check your app version in Settings → Info → “Version”. After withdrawing, you cannot use Aria until you consent again.
  • Deleting your account and data as well (any version): Settings → Account → “Delete account” or “Delete all my data”. This also ends transfers to AI providers.
  • People under 14 may not use Aria.

See the Generative AI Notice and Section 1.2 of our Privacy Policy.

How do I turn off ad measurement?

To measure our ads, the iPhone and iPad app (version 1.1 or later) tells Meta when the app is installed or opened and when a free trial or subscription starts. It never sends your name, email, account, advertising identifier (IDFA), or your tasks and chats. Turn off Settings → Privacy → “Ad Measurement” and that device sends nothing more; every feature keeps working. The setting applies to each device separately. The Mac app does not have this feature. See Sections 1.7 and 3 of our Privacy Policy.

How do I see or delete what the AI remembers?

Settings → “Memory” (on iPhone and iPad, in the account group at the top of Settings; on Mac, Settings → Account). In version 1.1.5 or earlier, Settings → AI → “Memory”.

How do I report an inappropriate AI reply?

In the chat, touch and hold the reply (on Mac, right-click) and choose “Report Inappropriate Response” — an email opens, pre-filled with the reply time and text. Or email support@letsaria.com with the date, time and content of the reply (a screenshot if possible).

How do I delete my account and data?

  • Signed in with Apple: Settings → Account → “Delete account”
  • Using a guest account: Settings → Account → “Delete all my data”

Your account and all server data are deleted immediately and cannot be recovered. If you use a guest account, delete your data before deleting the app; once the app is gone we have no way to find that guest account.

How do I request access to, correction of, or a stop to processing of my data?

Email our privacy officer (KIM HYUNGI, support@letsaria.com). We reply within 10 days. See Section 10 of our Privacy Policy.
