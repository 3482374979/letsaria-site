Meeting and Lecture Recording Summaries: Transcribe and Pull Out To-Dos with Aria

Last checked 2026-10-17 · Based on Aria 1.1.4 · Written by LIPSON, the maker of Aria.

Key answer Tap Start Recording in Aria to record a meeting or lecture. Aria summarizes the transcript and finds the to-dos in it. Add “Aria Recording” to Control Center and you can start recording right from the Lock Screen. Get everyone’s consent before you record, and check your school or company rules.

How do I record?

  1. Start recording Tap Start Recording in Aria.
  2. Stop when you are done Tap Stop Recording and the transcription and organizing begin.
  3. Check the result Review the transcript, the summary and the to-dos found in the recording.

If you start recording from an event, you can attach the summary to that event’s notes afterward.

How do I record straight from the Lock Screen?

Add “Aria Recording” to Control Center, and you can tap it to start recording even from the Lock Screen.

Where do recording files stay?

Recording files stay on the device that recorded them. If you need to transcribe again, do it on the device that made the recording.

What should I know before recording?

Aria uses AI to summarize and pull out to-dos. Get consent from everyone before you record, and check any school, workplace or local rules on recording. If other people are in the room, tell them you are recording first.
