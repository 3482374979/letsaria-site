To-Do List App for iPhone: Today, Upcoming, Repeats and Subtasks

Last checked 2026-10-17 · Based on Aria 1.1.4 · Written by LIPSON, the maker of Aria

Key answer Aria is an iPhone to-do list app that sorts tasks into four lists (Today, Upcoming, Someday and Inbox) and supports repeating tasks, subtasks and categories. Tasks you add by voice land in the same lists, and every non-AI task feature is free.

How are tasks organized into lists?

You see tasks in four lists: Today, Upcoming, Someday and Inbox. Tasks with a date also show on the calendar, so you do not have to switch between lists and events. The calendar side is covered in to-do calendar app.

How do you break up a big task?

Create subtasks inside a task to split it into small pieces. Tap Break it down with AI to get suggested steps, and set the due date, time and reminder lower on the same screen.

Can you set repeating tasks and categories?

Yes. You can use repeating tasks, categories and D-days. For things you do every day, a routine with a streak is easier.

Can you make a grocery list?

A grocery list groups items by category automatically. You can also show only the lists you pick to match a Focus.

How do you clean up overdue tasks?

In the evening, Aria points out what is left from today and what is past due, and Reschedule all moves them in one go. To move them one at a time, use Defer.

Frequently asked questions

Is Aria’s to-do list free?

Yes. Aria’s non-AI features, including lists, repeats and subtasks, are free. 20 AI questions a day are free too.

Can I set a task to repeat?

Yes. Aria supports repeating tasks, and a routine is a good fit for things you do every day.

Can I move my existing reminders into Aria?

Yes. Use Settings › General › Import › Import Apple Reminders. See Move tasks from Apple Reminders to Aria.
