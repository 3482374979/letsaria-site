Eisenhower Matrix Explained: Urgent vs. Important, with 16 Examples and App Setup

Last checked 2026-10-17 · Aria 1.1.4 · Written by LIPSON

Key answer The Eisenhower matrix sorts tasks with two questions, is it urgent and is it important, into four boxes: do now, schedule, reduce or delegate, and delete. The easiest box to neglect is important but not urgent, so put those tasks on your calendar first.

What is the Eisenhower matrix?

It is a prioritization grid named after U.S. President Dwight D. Eisenhower. Put urgency on one axis and importance on the other and you get four boxes.

  1. Box 1: urgent and important: do it now.
  2. Box 2: important but not urgent: set a date and time.
  3. Box 3: urgent but not important: reduce or delegate.
  4. Box 4: neither urgent nor important: delete.

Drawing the grid takes five minutes. The value comes from deciding what to do in each box.

How do I tell urgent from important?

  • Urgent? If you do not do it today or tomorrow, will you miss a deadline or leave someone waiting?
  • Important? If you skip it, will it make a difference to your work, studies or family goals a month from now?
  • Not sure? Put it in box 2. If it looks urgent but is someone else's request, double-check whether it belongs in box 3.

How do I move it into a to-do app?

If the four boxes stay on paper, you redraw them the next day. Move them into a to-do app's lists, alerts and calendar and each box turns directly into action.

FAQ

Do I need a spreadsheet or Notion template?

A cross drawn on paper is enough the first couple of times. Templates make the boxes look neat, but if you do not move the sorted tasks into dates and reminders, the list tends to stay on the page. If you already use a to-do app, moving things straight into its lists and calendar, as in the table above, is less work.

What if everything is urgent and important?

If more than five tasks landed in box 1, your standard is too loose. Write one line for each: what actually happens if I do not do this today? Move the ones with weak answers down to box 2 or 3.

What if I have nobody to delegate to in box 3?

If you work alone or are a student, read delegate as reduce. Answer messages twice a day in a batch, ask for the agenda before a meeting, and ask when a request is really due to cut the time you spend.
