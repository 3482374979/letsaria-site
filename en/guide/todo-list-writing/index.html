How to Write a To-Do List You Will Actually Finish

Last checked 2026-10-17 · Based on Aria 1.1.4 · Written by LIPSON, the maker of Aria.

Key answer Write each task as an action that starts with a verb and fits in one sitting, keep today’s list to three to five items, and give time-consuming tasks a slot on the calendar. Send what you can’t do today to a “someday” list so it is out of your head.

Why don’t to-do lists work?

There are usually three reasons. You wrote a topic, not a task (“report”). You wrote more than one day can hold. Or you never decided when to do it. A small change in how you write fixes all three.

How do I write one line of a to-do?

Write it so the next action is visible and your hand moves the moment you see it. Here are common entries rewritten.

  1. Write actions that start with a verb Instead of “report,” write “write a 5-line outline for the report,” so it is clear what to do.
  2. Shrink each task to one sitting If it won’t finish in 30 to 60 minutes, split it into subtasks.
  3. Keep today to 3-5 items One big task, one or two medium ones and a few small ones is plenty.
  4. Put time-consuming tasks on the calendar Deciding when is what makes it start.
  5. Re-sort leftovers in the evening Pick one of three: move to tomorrow, send to someday, or delete.

How many tasks should be on today’s list?

We suggest three to five. Meetings, messages and surprises will slip into the day, and you only keep trusting your list if you build up the experience of finishing everything on it. Don’t throw out the rest; give it a place.

  • Today — The 3-5 you must finish today
  • Upcoming — Tasks with a date
  • Someday — Things you want to do but have no date for
  • Inbox — Whatever comes to mind, to sort out later

How do I break a big task down?

Split a task that won’t finish in a day, like “prepare presentation,” into subtasks. For example: (1) write three questions the audience will ask, (2) draft slides 1-3, (3) check the numbers, (4) practice out loud once. In Aria, open the task and tap Break it down with AI to get suggested steps, and set the due date, time and reminder on the same screen.

What do I do with tasks I didn’t finish?

In the evening, look at what is left one by one and choose one of three: move it to tomorrow, remove the date and send it to someday, or delete it if you no longer need it. FAQ

Should I write my to-do list in the morning or the night before?

Either works. Many people draft it the night before from what is left, then trim it to three to five items in the morning once they see the day. Aria’s evening wrap-up and morning briefing follow this flow.

Is paper or an app better?

Paper helps you think while you write, and an app makes reminders, repeats and moving tasks easy. Mixing works well too: plan on paper, and put only what needs a reminder in the app (iPad planner apps).

Do I need a to-do list spreadsheet template?

A table is handy when several people track progress together. For your own daily tasks, an app with reminders and a Today list is often lighter to keep up.
