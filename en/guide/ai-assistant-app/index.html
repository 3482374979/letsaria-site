AI Assistant Apps Compared: General Chatbots, Device Assistants and Planner Assistants

Last checked 2026-10-17 · Based on Aria 1.1.4 · Written by LIPSON, the maker of Aria

Key answer AI assistant apps fall into three groups: general chatbots that answer almost anything (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude), device assistants tied to your phone’s own apps (Siri), and planner assistants that turn what you say into tasks and calendar events (Aria). If you only need answers, a chatbot is enough. If what you say has to end up as a reminder and a calendar event, a planner assistant fits better. Before you start, check where your history is kept.

This guide was written by LIPSON, the maker of Aria. Details about other apps come from their US App Store listings (descriptions, price and supported devices) and each company’s official pages as of October 17, 2026, and may change with updates. It is a guide to help you choose, not a ranking.

What should you check if you plan your day with a general chatbot?

Chatbots are strong at drafting plans and tidying lists. How they connect to reminders and your calendar depends on the service and the plan, and it changes often. Try these three things in the app you use:

  1. Say “remind me at 9 a.m. tomorrow.” Does your iPhone actually alert you?
  2. Does the schedule it built land in your iPhone calendar, or only stay in the chat window?
  3. A few days later, can you pull up just “this week’s to-dos” as a list?

What does a planner assistant do for you?

Take Aria as an example. One sentence like “Report due Friday, team meeting tomorrow at 3 p.m.” becomes one task and one event, with reminders set. In the morning, a briefing points out today’s events and what to do first; in the evening, one tap on Reschedule all moves what is left. Aria can also find dates in photos and screenshots and suggest events, and it can transcribe a meeting or lecture recording into a summary and to-dos (recording summaries). Get consent from everyone present before you record, and check your school or company rules first.

For questions that need current information, Aria searches the web and answers with sources; a task’s Look it up button works the same way. The full flow is in AI scheduling assistant.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free AI assistant app?

Yes. ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Aria are all free to download from the US App Store (checked October 17, 2026). What the free tier covers differs by service, and heavy use may need a paid plan. In Aria, 20 AI questions a day are free.

Does an AI assistant use my chats for training?

It depends on the service, so check each privacy policy and settings screen. Aria and its AI providers do not use your data to train AI models (see the FAQ).

How is Siri different from an AI assistant app?

Siri is the assistant built into iPhone, so it connects directly to built-in apps such as Reminders, Calendar and Phone. An app like Aria goes deeper on organizing tasks and events, and you can also call it from Siri and Shortcuts.
